On the Future of Modernization MegaPack

wyndchyme, 2026/10/18

Earlier today the GitHub repository for Modernization MegaPack, mc3ds-modern, was archived. This is because development has ceased on the pack. Significant advances on another mod, the details of which are not currently public, have made it clear that the MegaPack should be deprecated in favor of it. Additionally, modifications should use this new mod as a base as opposed to the MegaPack.

Modernization MegaPack was the flagship mod of this community from 2024 until this year. The decision to switch focus to the new mod was made after careful consideration of the benefits it would provide over the existing MegaPack. The MegaPack began development in a much more primitive time for New Nintendo 3DS Edition modding, and while updates have so far kept it in pace with advancements in the modding scene, the age and limitations of the pack make it impossible to keep the existing codebase in lockstep with the latest developments.

All existing users of Modernization MegaPack are strongly encouraged to switch to the new mod once it is released to the general public. All releases of Modernization MegaPack, including the beta releases of 3.2.0 "Lanquorwine" will remain avaliable on the archived repository. The MegaPack will no longer receive updates or support.

Thank you for supporting Modernization MegaPack. It is my hope that the community will unite around this new mod just as we united around the MegaPack for two years.
